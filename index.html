FAQ

Common Questions

Do you need my raw footage?

+

Both. I can work with raw footage from any camera, or edit files you already have — Premiere sequences, After Effects projects, or rough cuts you want elevated.

How long does a project take?

+

Short edits (60–90 sec): 2–4 days. Full brand videos: 1–2 weeks. Documentary: 2–3 weeks. Rush delivery available.

What is the free trial?

+

New clients can request a free sample edit on 30–60 seconds of their raw footage — no payment, no commitment. It's the fastest way to see NEURALX quality on your actual content before deciding.

How does the 50% advance work?

+

After our call and scope agreement, you pay 50% upfront to begin. The remaining 50% is due when you approve the final delivery. The advance is non-refundable once production has started.

What if I'm not happy with the result?

+

Unlimited revisions within the agreed scope until you're fully satisfied. I don't close a project until you love it.

Will this attract better-paying clients?

+

Yes — premium-looking content attracts premium clients. The Apple-style is immediately recognizable as high-end, and brands pay accordingly.

How long do you keep my files?

+

All files are securely stored for 40 days after delivery. After that window they are permanently deleted. Download everything you need as soon as you receive your files.

Is this for social content or long-form?

+

Both. Optimized exports for Instagram Reels, YouTube, TikTok, and long-form brand films. Every format at full quality.
